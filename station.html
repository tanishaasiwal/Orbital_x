THE ORBITAL PLATFORM

One station.
Many frontiers.

ORBITAL_X is a fictional commercial station concept: a place for independent research, international collaboration, and a new perspective on our home planet.

Its six connected modules are imagined around three essentials: purposeful work, restorative living, and an unobstructed view of Earth.
